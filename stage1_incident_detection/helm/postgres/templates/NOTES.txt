PostgreSQL is now running inside the cluster.

Internal DNS address (for Stage 2 RCA engine and the custom dashboard):

  Host:     postgres.postgres.svc.cluster.local
  Port:     5432
  Database: {{ .Values.auth.database }}
  User:     {{ .Values.auth.username }}

The rca_reports table was created automatically by the init script.
Indexes on triggered_at, failure_type, root_cause_service are ready.

To connect from your laptop:
  kubectl -n postgres port-forward svc/postgres 5432:5432
  psql -h localhost -U {{ .Values.auth.username }} -d {{ .Values.auth.database }}

To tail Postgres logs:
  kubectl -n postgres logs -f deployment/postgres

To inspect the schema:
  \d rca_reports
