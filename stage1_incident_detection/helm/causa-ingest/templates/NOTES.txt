Causa ingest trigger deployed!

Webhook endpoint (point Alertmanager here — it runs IN FRONT of Stage 2):
  http://{{ include "causa-ingest.fullname" . }}.{{ .Release.Namespace }}.svc.cluster.local:{{ .Values.service.port }}/trigger

Health check:
  http://{{ include "causa-ingest.fullname" . }}.{{ .Release.Namespace }}.svc.cluster.local:{{ .Values.service.port }}/health

It opens an incident window + records incident state in Postgres, then forwards
the webhook to STAGE2_WEBHOOK_URL ({{ .Values.env.STAGE2_WEBHOOK_URL }}).

Set the Alertmanager receiver:
  helm upgrade alertmanager helm/alertmanager \
    --set stage2WebhookUrl=http://{{ include "causa-ingest.fullname" . }}.{{ .Release.Namespace }}.svc.cluster.local:{{ .Values.service.port }}/trigger
